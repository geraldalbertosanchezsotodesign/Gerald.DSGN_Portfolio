Whiplash Poster

For this art publishing assignment we had to choose a song and make a poster of it.

I decided to talk about Caravan - Whiplash version, a version of the song created specifically for the film Whiplash, written and directed by Damien Chazelle, of which I am a big fan.

Academic Rigor Vs. Raw Improvisation


What fascinated me about this specific version written by Stan Getz, Duke Ellington, and other, just for the Whiplash Film, is its extreme speed and virtuosic execution compared to the original standard.

Rather than representing jazz through warm, spontaneous and expressive images, I wanted to capture the cold and competitive atmosphere of the elite conservatory to which the protagonist is admitted.

I deliberately stripped away colorful, organic improvisation in favor of structural discipline, focusing on the sheer technical perfection and relentless energy of the drummer.

220 BPM


I wanted the central visual element to be a direct mathematical translation of the music itself.

That's why I generated an optical pattern composed of exactly 220 vertical lines, directly translating the 220 BPM reached during the final drum solo (a massive leap from the 170–180 BPM of the original track).

By fine-tuning the spacing and density of these lines, I created a kinetic vibration that visually echoes the blur of moving drumsticks and the escalating tempo of the performance.
