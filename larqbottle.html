Larq Waterbottle

The primary objective of this project was to produce a promotional video through the 3D modeling and animation of a commercial product.

I selected Larq for its distinctive brand identity, developing a visual narrative heavily influenced by Apple's minimalist aesthetic.

Field
Motion Design
Academic Year
2025/2026
Role
Creative Director, Motion Designer & 3D Designer
Tools
Cinema 4D, After Effects, Premiere Pro

Hard work pays off.


This was probably the most complete project I've tackled solo, given the range of technical and organizational skills it demanded: from concepting to 3D modeling, all the way to audio/visual editing.

It let me consolidate my use of Cinema 4D for product animation, sharpen the workflow between After Effects and Premiere Pro, and learn to treat sound as an integral part of creative direction rather than an afterthought.

But above all, handling every phase of the process on my own taught me to hold a clear overview even as the work fragmented into dozens of small technical decisions.
