Love Yourself Poster
(Fight For Kindness)

This poster was developed for the “Fight for Kindness” global design contest.

The objective was to achieve a seamless integration of typography, graphic design and illustration, to effectively communicate the core values of altruism and the propagation of kindness.

I chose to represent a concept that is very close to my heart: starting with kindness toward yourself, in other words, learning to “Love Yourself”.
